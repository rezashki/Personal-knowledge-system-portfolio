Working principles

A system for learning—not just storing.

Active learning. Knowledge must support understanding, practice, or a decision.

Traceability. Sources, facts, assumptions, decisions, and actions stay distinct.

Useful connections. Linked notes make knowledge easier to rediscover in context.

Privacy by design. Confidential project material stays private; this page is a sanitized overview.
